<span class="doc-kind">How-to guide</span>

<Tabs syncKey="interface">
  <TabItem label="Ask your assistant">
    > How are my hours split between projects in September?

    `mp_stats {year: 2026, month: 9}` returns `totalHours`, `targetHours`, one row per project with `hours`, `days` and `share` (percent), and a `perDay` breakdown. Add `from` / `to` for a range or a single day.

    > Show me the whole grid.

    `mp_month {year: 2026, month: 9}` returns the projects (columns) and each day's hours per project. `mp_months {}` lists the months with status and totals.
  </TabItem>
  <TabItem label="Terminal">
    ```bash
    xflow-timesheet mp stats 2026-09                              # whole month
    xflow-timesheet mp stats 2026-09 --from 2026-09-08 --to 2026-09-12
    xflow-timesheet mp stats 2026-09 --from 2026-09-10 --to 2026-09-10   # one day
    xflow-timesheet mp month 2026-09                              # the full grid
    xflow-timesheet mp months                                     # months with status / totals
    ```
    ```
    2026-09-08 .. 2026-09-12: 40h booked of 40h target
    project                                    hours  days  share
    Administration (0077)                      30     5     75%
    AI Incubator - NovaLabs (900140) · 0081   10     5     25%
    ```
  </TabItem>
</Tabs>

The share is computed over the hours booked in the range, not over the target. For the Standard timesheet the same question is answered by [Check whether a month contains a jobcode](../check-jobcodes/).
