## What you can do with it

<CardGrid stagger>
  <Card title="See what is missing" icon="approve-check">
    Which working days already have hours, which still miss some, and how many hours are open in the month.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Book days in one go" icon="pencil">
    Set a day or a whole range to a jobcode or an absence type, fill the entire open period with one code, or apply your staffing plan.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Split time across projects" icon="list-format">
    Declare several projects per day, see the share each project holds, or rebalance two weeks to a 60/40 split.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Keep your password to yourself" icon="seti:lock">
    You sign in on your company's real login page, in a browser window the tool opens. Only the resulting SAP session is kept, on your machine.
  </Card>
</CardGrid>
